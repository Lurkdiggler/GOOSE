Our Studio

Telling your story with intention.

The Goose Creative is a full-service photography and film studio committed to telling your story with an editorial style of the highest caliber. We work with local, national, and international clients — bringing dedicated project management and design thinking to every shoot.

Based on Cape Cod and serving the South Coast, Providence, and all of New England, our work has been featured on Discovery Channel, Travel Channel, and in Boston Magazine.

linda@thegoosecreative.com
MP
Mike Perry
Creative Partner & Videographer

Self-taught the hard way — starting with slow-motion clips of his son's hockey games and years of nights on YouTube learning Final Cut, LUTs, and sound design until people started paying him for it. Sixteen years as a firefighter and paramedic bring a calm, steady presence to every set. His differentiator is sound: meticulous audio and color work that pulls a viewer all the way in, on every shoot.

mike@thegoosecreative.com
